The average hides the downside

Model, row mapping, and source notes

Fitted inputs. Class 7 - BG and CLV.xlsx: Beta Geometric (BG)!B1:B2 supplies gamma and delta. BG CLV!E1:E5 supplies AOF, AOV, margin, monthly contribution, and discount rate. Retention, survival, churn probability, discounting, and value formulas follow BG CLV!C9:J594.

Tail row. The visible table shows lifetimes 1 to 60 plus a 61+ aggregate. Its probability is S(60), and its displayed PAV and CLV are the probability-weighted conditional average of the remaining model tail. That keeps both expected-value routes complete without pretending the tail is a single lifetime.
