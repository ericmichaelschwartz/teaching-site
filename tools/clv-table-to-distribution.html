Both panels share one y-scale, so each dashed connector runs flat from a lifetime to its CLV twin: only the x-axis changes. The gold guides mark L = 12, 24, 36, 48 and 60 months: evenly spaced in months, but squeezed together in dollars, because discounting makes each extra year of lifetime add less. Everyone still here at n* = 1000 months is worth the maximum, max PAV less CAC, so they share one spike placed exactly there. Model, row mapping, and source notes

Fitted inputs. Class 7 - BG and CLV.xlsx: Beta Geometric (BG)!B1:B2 supplies a and b. BG CLV!E1:E5 supplies AOF, AOV, margin, monthly contribution, and discount rate. Retention, survival, churn probability, discounting, and value formulas follow BG CLV!C9:J594.

Tail row. The visible table shows lifetimes 1 to 60 plus a 61+ aggregate. Its probability is S(60), and its displayed PAV and CLV are the probability-weighted conditional average of the remaining model tail, so the tail is not treated as a single lifetime.
