MKT 419/626 — Customer-Base Analysis

The Customer Lifetime Value Timeline

Explore how retention, margin, discounting, and acquisition cost combine into customer lifetime value — value to date, residual value, and break-even.

Cohort Revenue Decomposition

Follow one cohort's revenue through the decomposition tree: six linked charts covering active customers, orders, and spend, with count/percent and calendar/tenure views. Hover any chart and every panel lights up at the same point in time.

Cohort Unit Economics DCF

Build discounted cash flow for one acquisition cohort step by step: cash per active customer, margin, percent active, and discounting, down to payback and ROI. Then line up every cohort on one chart to see how the economics shift.
